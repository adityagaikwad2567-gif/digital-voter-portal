Any person who is 18 years or older and a citizen of India can register in this academic demonstration system. This is not an official government portal.

Go to Voter Services > Search Electoral Roll. You can search by name, demo voter ID, or mobile number using demonstration data.

Navigate to Voter Services > Update / Correction and submit a correction request with the required details. You will receive a reference number for tracking.

Use Voter Services > Find Polling Station. Search by your demo voter ID, name, district, or constituency to find your assigned demo polling station.

The digital voter card is a demonstration-only digital representation of voter information. It is not an official government ID and cannot be used for official purposes.

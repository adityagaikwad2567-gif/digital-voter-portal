Who Can Register as a Voter?

In this academic demonstration system, the following eligibility criteria apply:

  • You must be 18 years of age or older (as of January 1st of the current year).
  • You must be a citizen of India (declaration-based in this demo).
  • You must not already be registered in the demo electoral roll.
  • You must provide a valid residential address within a recognized demo constituency.
These are demonstration rules for academic purposes only and do not represent actual Indian electoral law.

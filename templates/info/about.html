Digital Voter Services & Online Voting Portal

This Digital Voter Services & Online Voting Portal is an academic project developed by Aditya Gaikwad and Aditi Naik as part of the BCA Second Year curriculum. It demonstrates the types of voter-management services commonly associated with electoral systems, including voter registration, electoral-roll search, polling-station information, digital voter-card display, correction requests, application tracking, election information, controlled online voting, results, notifications, and administration.

This is an academic demonstration project. It is not an official government website and should not be used for any official electoral purposes.
